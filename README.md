# EastWestCardGame2

Location-based iOS card game. The player's side (East or West) is determined by thier GPS location relative to a fixed longitude point. Features like dark mode, sount effercts and background music


## How to play
    1. Open the app and insert your name
    2. Allow location access - the app assigns you east or west based on position
    3. Press START to begin
    4. Each round - 5 seconds countdown -> two cards are reveled for 3 seconds 
    5. The player with the higher cards win this round (Ace is highest)
    6. After 10 rounds the player with the most points wins
